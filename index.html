Built on the ground, one machine at a time

A UAE-owned fleet trusted by the region's biggest contractors.

Orient World Construction Equipment Trading & Rental L.L.C. was founded in 2017, starting with a small fleet and a simple commitment: never let a contractor's schedule stall for lack of machinery. That commitment has scaled into one of the busiest rental operations in the Emirates.

From 2020 to today, the fleet has averaged over 200 machines deployed across active sites every single month — through infrastructure, solar, marine and civil projects — without missing that pace once.
